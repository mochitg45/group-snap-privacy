Privacy Policy — Group Snap: Cute Animal Puzzle

Effective 2 October 2026 (updated for iOS, Apple Game Center and tracking choices). Applies to the Android app and the iPhone and iPad app, both with the identifier com.inatasunsoft.groupsnap, published by Inata Sun Soft.

Group Snap is a single-player puzzle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not send your progress or your photos anywhere. This policy explains what stays on your device and the small amount of data that the advertising service inside the app handles.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress (levels, stars, coins, streaks and settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

Camera and photos (Friends Snap)

Friends Snap is an optional mode where you can put your friends' faces on the animals. To do that:

  • Camera: if you tap the camera button, the app asks for camera permission and shows a live preview inside the game. The picture is taken on your device, cropped to a small face circle, and the camera is switched off straight away. Nothing from the camera is uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Gallery: instead of the camera you can pick a photo from your device. Only the face area you crop is kept.
  • Storage: cropped faces, the names you type and the Friends Snap photos you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete them at any time from "My friends" ("Delete all friend photos"), and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Save to phone and Share: a finished Friends Snap photo leaves the app only when you choose to: "Save to phone" puts it in your device's photo gallery, and "Share" hands it to the app you pick in your device's share sheet. What happens after that is up to you and that app.

Please only add photos of people who are happy for you to use their picture.

Advertising choices

iPhone and iPad: the first time the game starts, Apple asks whether Group Snap may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can decline; the game plays exactly the same and ads are then not personalised with your Advertising ID. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is also shown, and you can reopen it from "Privacy options" in the game's Settings.

Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad request, and you can reopen it from "Privacy options" in the game's Settings.

Cloud save

Cloud save (progress that follows you to a new device) exists on Android only, through Google Play Games Services. The iPhone and iPad app has no cloud save: your progress stays on the device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising service above, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google.

Your rights and how to request data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app stores is on your device and can be deleted in the app ("My friends" → "Delete all friend photos") or by uninstalling it. For data held by AdMob, reset your Advertising ID or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. To manage or delete your Play Games profile, scores and cloud saves, go to play.google.com/games/profile. To manage your Game Center profile and scores, use Settings → Game Center on your iPhone or iPad, or contact Apple.
